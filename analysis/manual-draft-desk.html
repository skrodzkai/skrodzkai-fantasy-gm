How to read this board

Value is projected points above the board’s replacement baseline, not a made-up 0–100 grade. Missing values stay blank. ADP is market timing context, not a promise a player will survive. Injury flags require a current check; absence of a Yahoo marker is not medical clearance.

IDP points may be a calibrated decision score rather than raw season totals; player details preserve the distinction. This desk does not reproduce the live automation’s VONA engine or certify roster legality.
